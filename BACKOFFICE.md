# Back-office routine

## Daily (2 minutes)
- iPad: passcode on, auto-lock 2 minutes, nobody else uses the clinic iPad with your login.
- New patient: ask for consent and tick the box before saving. If you will use "Read with AI" on their reports,
  mention that report photos are read by an AI service.
- Check every value the AI reads before tapping "Add to investigations".

## Weekly (5 minutes, e.g. Saturday evening)
- Clinic settings → Make a backup copy (includes scan photos, so it grows over time). Keep it in a private
  Google Drive / iCloud folder.
  Keep the last 8 weekly copies; delete older ones.
- Open the newest backup once a month in "Restore" on a second device to confirm it works.

## Monthly
- Remove staff access for anyone who has left (later, when staff logins exist).
- Check the WhatsApp and hosting bills against expectations (Phase 2).

## Yearly
- Review the privacy notice at the reception desk.
- Renew the web address (domain).

## If something goes wrong
- iPad lost or stolen: erase it with Find My iPad, then restore the latest backup on a new device.
- You suspect patient data leaked: note what happened and when; under the DPDP Rules a personal-data
  breach must be reported to the Data Protection Board and to affected patients without delay (72 hours
  for the Board's detailed report). Get legal help the same day.
- App behaves oddly: stop, make a backup copy first, then open a Claude session and describe the problem.

## Notice for the reception desk (suggested text)
"We keep your health records digitally to treat you and send your prescription on WhatsApp. Records are
kept securely and shared only with your consent or as required by law. You may ask to see, correct or
delete your information. Contact: <clinic phone>."
